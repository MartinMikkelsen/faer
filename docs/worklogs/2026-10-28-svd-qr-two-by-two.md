# Bidiagonal QR two-by-two exit (#81)

## Decisions

- Handle a remaining two-by-two block directly. The reported near-repeated pair can leave QR cycling at machine precision rather than decreasing its off-diagonal. The existing deflation tolerance and iteration limit are retained; neither numerical checkers nor production tolerances are relaxed.
- Use faer's existing `JacobiRotation` operations. For the current lower-bidiagonal block B, a Givens rotation G symmetrizes G B, then a symmetric Jacobi rotation J diagonalizes it. Accumulate `G^T J` into U and J into V. The ordinary final sign and ordering passes retain their existing role. The block is already scaled by QR's existing normalization, keeping the scalar construction bounded.
- Recover the smaller signed value as `(f / larger) * h` from `det(B) = f*h`. Dividing first avoids cancellation in the small eigenvalue and premature loss of precision in the product f*h. The reduced failure, sign cases, relative small-value checks and an explicit subnormal result exercise this choice. Reuse the existing deflation/iteration-error path to preserve bounded iteration and error-state rescaling.
- [LAPACK 3.12.1 DBDSQR](https://netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/d6/d51/group__bdsqr_gade20fbf9c91aa7de0c3d565b39588dc5.html) also has a direct two-by-two exit through DLASV2. No LAPACK code is ported here; the construction above uses the existing faer rotations and the two-by-two algebra. The reporter's suggestion of a broader tolerance safety factor was not adopted.
- Keep the reporter's 12x10 complex fixture, including all twelve tiny noise entries. Its nonzero f64 round-trip decimals are unchanged; only its sixty explicit zero entries are omitted from storage. Credit and source are recorded at the fixture. Check residuals, orthogonality, ordering and the leading spectrum without comparing individual vectors in the repeated subspaces. The near-zero singular values use an absolute error bound because different LAPACK drivers/providers do not establish their relative accuracy at this scale.
- Scope is SVD's bidiagonal QR path, including divide-and-conquer QR leaves and the real core used by complex adapters. Scratch requirements, the public API, recursion thresholds and scheduling are unchanged. SVD campaign acceptance under the adoption protocol is still required before merge; no performance claim is made.

## Verification conclusions and constraints

- Baseline: current fork main `2592bad8cef0bbba00d1ae31c0442ea6cf3c5310`, Homebrew Rust 1.98.1, aarch64-apple-darwin, optimized development profile. The separate #73 and #74 worktrees were preserved and are not included. The internal 3x3 near-repeated-pair reproducer returns `NoConvergence` before this change and passes afterward. The reporter's full 12x10 fixture passes before and after on this build; the reported Rust 1.99.0 release build was not reproduced. The internal case is the local failure proof.
- The focused tests complete 97 real-kernel calls (f32/f64, near-repeated and sign cases, four scales and four output combinations, plus the determinant-order subnormal check) and 128 public complex calls (c32/c64, four scales, A and its adjoint, default/maximal QR thresholds and four output choices). They check reconstruction and orthogonality where vectors are requested, finite/nonnegative/ordered spectra, and relative accuracy for small prescribed singular values.
- Independent SciPy 1.13.0 / NumPy 1.26.4 LAPACK `gesvd` and `gesdd` checks of the exact fixture agree on the leading eight singular values and reconstruct it with relative residual about 8.2e-16. This confirms the fixture and numerical oracle; it does not establish identical low-level rounding paths across compilers.
- The ordinary workspace run reports 461 faer passes and 138 ignored tests. Its three failures are unchanged from baseline: `linalg::matmul::internal::tests::empty_strict_triangle_preserves_parent_guards`, `linalg::matmul::internal::tests::strict_triangle_maps_preserve_stored_entries_and_parent_guards`, and `linalg::matmul::internal::tests::strict_triangle_preserves_nonempty_parent_guards`.
- The SVD selection with ignored LAPACK groups reports 75 passes and the same three existing failing groups: f32/c32 `rank_one_at_recursion_splits` (#73 scratch panics) and f32 `bidiagonal_family` (#75 V orthogonality). All 18,172 configured LAPACK calls are attempted. There are two existing call failures and three additional orthogonality diagnostics, with no new failing groups. The earlier baseline SVD run at the same revision is retained with the evidence.
- All 25 public integration tests, 60 documentation tests and the std-only build pass. These use the final production implementation; the final ordinary run additionally includes the last test-only subnormal assertion. Stable rustfmt reports exactly the same three legacy differences as baseline, with no new formatting differences. The diff whitespace check passes. Detailed commands, logs, fixture verification and the full source patch are in the [evidence archive](evidence/2026-10-28-svd-qr-two-by-two.tar.gz).
- No upstream faer checkout was measured and no upstream feedback is part of this work. The full opt-in non-SVD LAPACK suite, other platforms, the reporter's release/toolchain combination and benchmark acceptance remain unverified. This is not an adopted or released change.
